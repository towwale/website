# Realism plan (12 Nov 2026)

Why the last build looked broken: the truck and cars are detailed scanned models, but
everything built around them in code (ground, yard, tyres, battery, key) was plain blocks
and flat colour. The bike was a low-detail game model. Load changes and lane changes were
animated mechanically.

1. Slant: lane change long and gentle; the turn into the yard a proper curve.
2. Load changes: a smooth dissolve from one load to the next. No drop, no scale, no bounce.
3. Lighting and ground: light from a real photographed sky (HDRI); real photographed
   asphalt, earth and concrete for road, verge and yard. Source: Poly Haven, CC0.
4. Props: no hand-built props. Scanned real objects from Poly Haven (old_tyre,
   metal_jerrycan, plastic_jerrycan, metal_toolbox, Barrel_01, concrete_road_barrier,
   modular_chainlink_fence, rollershutter_door).
5. Bike: a high-detail motorcycle model from Sketchfab.
6. Yard: Renafox's photo-scanned "Wall of Cars", scanned wrecks, chain-link fence, barrels
   and tyre piles on real concrete.

Later: the 6-wheel truck, real toll plaza and town, route line, phone layout.
